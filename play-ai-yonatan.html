HOW TO PLAY

EARTH: Drag to rotate the planet. Catch objects in the hole! Hitting the solid donut is GAME OVER.

COINS & SHOPS: Earn coins to buy power-ups and unlock secret Arcades! The arcade machines hide other dimensions.

BOSS FIGHTS: Survive long enough to trigger epic space boss battles!

CONTROLS: Drag mouse/finger to rotate the world. In minigames, tap/click/space to play.
